# Funding mechanism reference table

| Mechanism | Best for | Typical scale | Lead time |
|---|---|---|---|
| NSTC general research grant | Technical novelty, PI-driven | NT$1–3M/yr × 2–3 yr | 6–9 mo |
| NSTC AI Major Program | Industry-facing, deliverable-heavy | NT$10M+/yr | 9–12 mo |
| NHRI 整合型計畫 | Multi-PI clinical translation | NT$5–15M/yr × 3–5 yr | 9–12 mo |
| NHRI 個人型計畫 | Single-PI, mid-career, clinical | NT$2–4M/yr | 6–9 mo |
| MOHW 衛福部科技計畫 | Policy-relevant, outcomes-driven | NT$3–8M/yr | 6–9 mo |
| MOHW 緊急醫療政策計畫 | Trauma / EMS / ED-system research | Variable | 6 mo |
| Industry partnership (聯發科 / 廣達 / GE / Philips TW) | Co-development, IP-shared | Variable | Negotiable |
| NIH R21 (US) | Exploratory, 2-year | USD 275k total | 9 mo |
| NIH R01 (US) | Mature, 4–5 year | USD 250–500k/yr | 9–12 mo |
| EU Horizon | Consortium, multi-country | EUR 2–10M | 12 mo |
| TFDA SaMD pre-submission | Regulatory pathway clarification | N/A (not a grant) | 3–6 mo |

## How to use this table

For each candidate:
1. Identify a **primary** funding target (highest fit on call topics + scale + lead time)
2. Identify **one viable backup** (different agency in case primary call closes / cycles)
3. Both targets must be named; "applicable to multiple grants" is not specific enough

## Heuristics for fit

- High technical novelty + PI-driven → NSTC general
- Large multi-center clinical translation → NHRI 整合型 or MOHW
- Industry partner ready / deliverable required → NSTC AI Major or industry direct
- Trauma / EMS / ED-system → MOHW 緊急醫療政策計畫 (specific call)
- Exploratory pilot → NSTC general or NIH R21
- Mature program with extensive prelim data → NIH R01 or NHRI 整合型 multi-year

## Important caveat

Funding agency call cycles change. Verify call topic and timing before final commitment. The lead times above are typical-case; in election or budget-cycle years they can stretch by 2–3 months.
